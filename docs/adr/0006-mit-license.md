# MIT license

dendrograph is MIT licensed, choosing adoption over control.

## Considered Options

AGPL-3.0 was seriously considered, on the argument that the most obvious commercial
move available to a third party — wrapping dendrograph as a hosted "connect your
GitHub, get your graph" service — inverts the product's core promise that no data
reaches someone else's server, while keeping its name. AGPL's network-use copyleft
blocks exactly that, and would cost little in practice since the distribution model
is forking and running it yourself.

It was rejected anyway: AGPL deters corporate contributors and some contributors
avoid it reflexively, and the project would rather have contributors than a
guarantee. The trade-off is accepted knowingly, so nobody needs to reopen it.
